Thanks for the quick questions. I pulled one of the bolts off the existing line and measured it — answers below:

  • Brackets are stainless steel, bolting onto structural steel framework
  • It's a through-bolted connection, not tapped
  • Holes take roughly a 1/2" fastener
  • Grip works out to about 2.5–3" with the bracket flange and framing
  • Whole area is a washdown zone — corrosion resistance matters to us
  • Loading is moderate / structural, nothing extreme
existing-fastener-measured.jpg
Existing bolt off the running line, measured against a tape — reads right around 3".

This is the one we currently run. Whatever you'd recommend to match it for the new installation works for us.
